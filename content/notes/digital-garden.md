A **digital garden** is a personal website organised around ideas and the links between them, rather than around chronology. A blog is a stream — newest at the top, posts finished and forgotten. A garden is a network — notes are added, revised, cross-linked, and allowed to mature in public.

Three things tend to be true of digital gardens:

- **Notes over posts.** Short, focused pieces that cover one idea. No pressure to be comprehensive.
- **Links over dates.** Navigation happens by following connections between ideas, not scrolling a timeline.
- **Growth over perfection.** Notes are published early and revised often. A 🌱 seedling is welcome alongside a 🌳 evergreen.

This garden uses [Kavu](https://github.com/stultus/kavu), a Hugo theme built for this style of publishing. Each note carries a maturity badge so readers can tell whether they're reading a half-formed thought or something stable.
