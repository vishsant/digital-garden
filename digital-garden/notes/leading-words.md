A leading word is a compact term that points an agent toward a larger engineering pattern. Examples include tracer bullet, vertical slice, deep module, ubiquitous language, feedback loop, and gardening.

Using a term repeatedly can activate related concepts from the model's learned associations. “Tracer bullet” can point toward thin end-to-end implementation, early integration, visible progress, and fast feedback.

Leading words are not magic commands. They do not replace project-specific requirements or verification. Their deeper value is vocabulary: a good term compresses shared reasoning and reduces ambiguity.
